Build safely

Building a computer is safe when you take a little care. This is a general guide, not instructions for your particular parts.

  • Unplug first. Shut the computer down, switch the power supply off and unplug its cord before you touch anything inside the case.
  • Mind static. Static electricity can damage parts without any visible sign. Touch bare metal on the case before handling parts, hold them by the edges, keep them in their bags until you fit them, and don't build on carpet. A wrist strap helps.
  • Never open a power supply, even when it's unplugged. It can hold a dangerous charge.
  • Use the right cables. Only use the modular cables that came with your power supply, and push every power plug in until it clicks.
  • Your manuals come first. Wherever they differ from this site, follow the manual for your part.
  • Take your time with the socket. Most sockets have tiny pins that bend easily (on AM4 the pins are on the processor instead). Keep the socket's cover on until the processor goes in, and never force anything.
