# Privacy Policy

**Wreckage of the Namtar**. Last updated: November 2026

## Summary

Wreckage of the Namtar does not collect, transmit, or share any personal
data. Your saves stay on your device. The only network activity is Google
Play handling the in-app purchase, which is done entirely by Google.

## Data Collection

The game collects **no data whatsoever**. No personal information, no
analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers, no
location data. There is no account system, no server, and no cloud component
operated by the developer.

## Data Storage

Your saves (position, inventory, keys and map progress), your settings, and
the record of your purchase are written to the game's private app storage on
your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them. The game also writes short
lines to the Android system log on your device: one when it starts (the game
version, the Android version and the device model) and one when something
goes wrong, naming the failure but never the contents of a save. The game
never sends these lines anywhere.

## Backups

Saves, settings and the purchase record may be included in the standard
Android system backup and device-to-device transfer, so progress can be
restored when you move to a new phone. That backup is operated by Android and
your Google account, not by the developer, and is covered by Google's own
privacy policy.

## Security

There is no server, account, or database to breach. Nothing is collected, so
there is nothing held anywhere for an attacker to reach. On your device, saves
are written to the game's private app storage, which the Android application
sandbox keeps readable only by this game. Purchase verification happens
inside Google Play, so payment details never reach the game at all.

## Purchases

Act 1 is free. The acts after it are paid, unlocked by **in-app purchase**.
See the [game page](namtar.html) for release status. The purchase is
processed **entirely by Google Play**:

- The developer never sees, collects, or stores your payment details, such as
  card numbers, billing address, or real name.
- The game only learns whether you own the unlock, and stores that answer on
  your device.
- The purchase is tied to your Google account, so Google Play can restore it
  if you reinstall or switch devices.
- Payment and transaction data is handled under
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the
  Google Play Terms of Service. Refunds are handled through Google Play.

There are **no ads** and no subscriptions.

## Network Access and Permissions

The game itself makes no network requests. It includes Google's Play Billing
library, which automatically adds the `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, and
`com.android.vending.BILLING` permissions. These are used solely to
communicate with Google Play about the purchase. No gameplay data, saves, or
device information is ever transmitted.

The only other permission requested is **Vibration** (`VIBRATE`), used for
haptic feedback in play. The one remaining manifest entry is a private
signature permission Android adds automatically to keep the game's own
internal components from being triggered by other apps. It grants no access to
your data or to any device feature. No location, camera, microphone, contacts,
or storage permissions are requested or used.
