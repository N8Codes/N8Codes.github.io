Privacy Policy

Wreckage of the Namtar — Last updated: September 2026

Summary

Wreckage of the Namtar does not collect, transmit, or share any data. The game has no internet permission at all, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Your saves stay on your device.

Data Collection

The game collects no data whatsoever. No personal information, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers, no location data. There is no account system, no server, and no cloud component operated by the developer.

Data Storage

Your saves — position, inventory, keys, map progress and settings — are written to the game's private app storage on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Backups

Saves may be included in the standard Android system backup and device-to-device transfer, so progress can be restored when you move to a new phone. That backup is operated by Android and your Google account, not by the developer, and is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Security

There is no server, account, or database to breach: nothing is collected, so there is nothing held anywhere for an attacker to reach. On your device, saves are written to the game's private app storage, which the Android application sandbox keeps readable only by this game.

Network Access and Permissions

The game requests a single permission: Vibration (VIBRATE), used for haptic feedback in play. It does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot make network requests of any kind. No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions are requested or used.

Purchases

Act 1 is free. The acts after it are paid, unlocked by in-app purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees or stores your payment details. There are no ads. See the game page for release status.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to N8Codes at n8codesandcreates@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
