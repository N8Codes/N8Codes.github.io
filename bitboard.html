BitBoard

A battle map for your tabletop RPG, right on your phone.

In development for Android™, not yet released. Features can change before it ships. What will not change: free, no ads, no tracking, fully offline.

Your game table

Lay down the grid, drop in your party and the monsters, and roll. Then, when the session is over, sit across from a friend for a classic.

A grid for any map

A grid over a table, or over your own image, like a dungeon map. Pinch to zoom and drag to move.

Tokens your way

Shapes with labels, your own photos, or any piece from the game sets and themes.

Dice from d4 to d100

Up to twenty at a time, each kind in its own shape and colour.

Four classics for two

Chess, checkers, go on 9×9, 13×13 or 19×19, and backgammon with the doubling cube. Rules built in.

Theme packs

Fifteen of them, from knights and samurai to underwater and sci-fi, or mix pieces, boards and backgrounds yourself.

Fully offline

No account, no internet permission, nothing collected. See the privacy policy for the details.
