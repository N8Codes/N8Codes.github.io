NutCracker

A cozy pixel-art match-3 puzzle. Crack nuts, dodge obstacles, and outwit Nutkin — a sneaky squirrel who steals your pieces and occasionally leaves a snack behind when he feels guilty.

NutCracker for mobile coming soon

Chapters 1–5 (25 levels) are free · one purchase unlocks chapters 6–10 forever · no ads, no subscriptions.

How it plays

Swap and match walnuts, almonds, pecans, and six more nut types. Line up four or more — or make L, T, and square shapes — to charge special nuts that clear rows, blast areas, or wipe a whole nut type off the board.

10 pixel worlds, 50 levels

Journey from Spring Forest to the Haunted House. Each world brings its own nuts, backdrops, and obstacles — ice, stone, vines, and spreading rot.

Nutkin the squirrel

Every few turns he scampers across your board — swiping a nut, shuffling your pieces, or leaving a bonus snack. Watch his telegraph and plan around him.

Rescue power-ups

Match beside obstacles to earn power-ups that shatter all the ice, quake every stone, whip away the vines, or cure the rot in one move.

Earn every star

Timed and move-based levels with a 5-star scoring system and per-level high scores. Replay any level to beat your best.

Fully offline

No account, no internet needed to play, and your progress stays on your device. See the privacy policy for the details.

Buy once, own it

Chapters 1–5 are completely free. A single in-app purchase unlocks chapters 6–10 forever — no ads, no subscriptions, no energy timers.
