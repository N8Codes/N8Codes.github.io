Privacy Policy

PC builder and the Build a computer guide (pc-builder.html, learn-pc*.html) — Last updated: November 2026

Summary

The PC builder runs entirely in your browser. Your build stays on your device: nothing you choose or type is sent to N8Codes or to anyone else. There is no account, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and the page keeps nothing after you close it. The guide pages are plain pages with no scripts at all.

Data Collection

The developer collects nothing. The pages have no server of their own, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. The builder contains no code that sends anything over the network, and its security policy tells your browser to block any such request. It doesn't use cookies or store anything in your browser.

Hosting

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, when your browser loads a page, GitHub receives the usual request details, such as your IP address and browser type, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. N8Codes doesn't receive those details. The pages load nothing from any other site.

Your Build

  • On screen. Your build exists only in the open page. Closing or reloading the page discards it unless you saved it; the page warns you before you leave with changes you haven't saved.
  • Your own parts. Numbers you type for "My own part" stay in the page, and in your saved file if you save one.
  • Save. "Save as .txt" creates a file in your browser and saves it with your browser's normal download. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Load. "Load a .txt" reads a file you choose, in your browser only. The page reads it as data and keeps only the parts and values it recognises. The file is never sent anywhere.
  • Print. "Print my cards" uses your browser's own print dialog.

Links to Other Sites

A real part's card links to its maker's own product page, and the builder can open a guide page in a new tab. Following a link takes you to that site, under its own privacy policy; nothing about your build is sent with it.
