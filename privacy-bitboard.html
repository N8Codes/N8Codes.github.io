Privacy Policy

BitBoard — Last updated: September 2026

Summary

BitBoard does not collect, transmit, or share any data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Your games, boards, photos, and settings stay on your device, and in the standard Android system backup if you use it.

Data Collection

BitBoard collects no data whatsoever. No personal information, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers, no location data. There is no account system, no server, and no cloud component operated by the developer.

Data Storage

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device: your games in progress and your game history, your Bit Board boards and the photos you chose for them, your appearance and display settings, and a random number made on your device that is used only to help shuffle dice rolls.

Deleting a board removes it at once. Photos that only that board used are removed the next time the board list is opened. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this local data.

Photos You Choose

Bit Board lets you pick a photo from your device to use as a token or as a board background. You choose it through the Android system photo picker, or the system file picker on older versions of Android, so BitBoard never has access to your photo library, only to the single image you pick.

The app keeps its own copy of that image, reduced in size if it is large, and saved without the original file's metadata, such as location. The app never uploads it anywhere.

Backups

Your games, game history, boards, the photos you chose, and your settings may be included in the standard Android system backup and device-to-device transfer, so they can be restored on a new phone. The dice number is left out. This backup is operated by Android and your Google account, not by the developer, and is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Security

There is no server, account, or database to breach: nothing is collected, so there is nothing held anywhere for an attacker to reach. On your device, your data is written to the app's private storage, which the Android application sandbox keeps readable only by BitBoard itself.

Network Access and Permissions

BitBoard does not request the internet permission, so it cannot make network requests of any kind. It requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions. Its only permission entry is a private signature permission, added automatically by an AndroidX library, which keeps the app's own internal components from being triggered by other apps. It grants no access to your data or to any device feature.

There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to N8Codes at n8codesandcreates@gmail.com.

Changes
