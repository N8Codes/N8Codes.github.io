PC builder

Build a computer, one part at a time. Click a dashed outline to see what that part does and choose one, or press Random build for a computer that already fits; the checks tell you whether everything fits. Drag to turn the computer, Shift+drag or right-drag to move it, and scroll to zoom in where you point. Arrow keys turn it, Shift+arrows move it, + and − zoom; ⌂ resets the view. Press Together: when every part fits, the case closes, the computer stands up and your cards appear.
